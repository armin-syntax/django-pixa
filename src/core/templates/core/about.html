About Pixa

Find images worth keeping.

Pixa is a visual discovery platform built around photography, personal collections and simple sharing. People can upload their own photos, discover work from others, save images they love and build a profile that reflects their taste.

The experience takes inspiration from the visual discovery of Pinterest and the photography-first spirit of Unsplash, while keeping the interface quieter and more focused. Images, creators and small details are given room to breathe.

Every photo can have a title, a short caption and tags, making it easier to discover related images and return to the things that inspire you.
